Teams & Missions

Draw a mission. Make magic. 🎯

Once we're in the rhythm, each older kid leads a small team of younger kids. Every team gets a secret mission — and a few months to bring it to life.

How a mission works

From the draw to the big day

🎟️

1. Draw the mission

Each team pulls a country or theme from the mission jar — that's their world for the next few months.

🔍

2. Research & story

Where is it? What do they eat? Why? Teams dig into history, geography, and culture.

🖍️

3. Build a poster

Bright, bold, beautiful — a visual journey through their country and its food.

🎤

4. Give a talk

Teams present their story to the group — older kids coach younger ones to shine.

🍳

5. Cook the dish

Together, they prepare the dish for everyone to taste.

🏆

6. Final showcase

Posters, presentations, plates — and friendly judging to celebrate every team's journey.

👧🧒👦

Why kid-led?

When older kids teach, they learn twice. When younger kids are taught by someone closer to their age, they listen differently — and dream bigger. That mix is the secret sauce of Taste the World.

🧭

What we're building toward

We're starting simple — older kids teaching younger ones one dish at a time. As we grow, teams will take on longer, deeper missions, and the whole community will come together to share, celebrate, and taste the world.

🚀
