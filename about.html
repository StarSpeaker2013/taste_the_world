About

Why we taste the world 🌎

Because food is never just food — it's history, geography, science, and human connection on a plate.

✨

Our mission

Help kids fall in love with the world by exploring it one dish at a time. We tell the stories behind food — where it came from, who carried it, how it changed history — and then we cook it together.

🧑‍🍳

Every month: an in-person event

Older kids take the lead — they tell the story of a country's food and teach younger kids how to make it. We taste, we share, we learn.

🏆

Then: teams & missions

Once we have a rhythm, each older kid will lead a small team of younger kids. Each team draws a mission — a country, a theme, a challenge — and spends a few months on it: posters, presentations, cooking, and a final celebration where we share, judge, and cheer.

🎉

Special days

We'll also host themed celebrations — International Day, China Day, Mexico Day, and more — full of food, music, and stories from around the globe.

🤝

Want to be part of it?

Whether you want to cook, tell a story, or just taste — there's a seat at the table for you.
